Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide ExpenseTrack, we collect:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, needed to keep the service running.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate and improve ExpenseTrack: to authenticate you, store and display your expenses, generate your spending summaries, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

We do not share your expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information when you visit. We may also be required to disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@expensetrack.example.
